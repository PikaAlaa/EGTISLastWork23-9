## Task 1: decision rule and recall cost

Separate tested functions detect URL-side evidence, a request for a secret, and an urgent action combined with an Egyptian mobile number or short link. Keyword data cover English, Arabic and Egyptian-dialect forms. Both local mobile formatting and +20 international formatting are recognized. Existing exact blacklist and decisive brand/userinfo rules retain priority; existing INCOMPLETE precedence is preserved. A high text score with no corroboration produces MEDIUM.

The URL score remains inspectable. A host-only model signal is suppressed as standalone corroboration on an exact official-domain registry match or a host in the existing shared-hosting registry, uniformly across brands. This prevents host-level labels from treating every hosted document or every official subdomain as phishing. A raw URL-model alert plus a secret request can still produce HIGH; exact blacklist and decisive brand evidence still apply. There is no exception keyed to an individual bank and no Tranco allowlist in the packaged artifact. This is an integration qualification to the general “URL model alone → MEDIUM” rule, and its false-negative consequences remain unmeasured on real traffic.

**82 of 93 phishing validation messages move from HIGH to MEDIUM** under the unchanged deployed text model. Language counts: {"ar": 48, "mixed": 34}. Source counts: {"salloum_gaber_bilingual_corpus": 18, "kaggle_arabic_phishing_fully_dataset": 30, "kaggle_dialectal_arabic_sms": 34}. The calculation uses an isolated empty reputation database and the configured brand registry. It measures loss of HIGH severity; these messages still have a MEDIUM alert. It is not a population recall estimate.

## Task 2: URL data, models and bias

Tranco list **PY35J**, dated **2026-10-08**, is pinned at its first 20,000 domains. The Public Suffix List snapshot includes private entries and is read locally through tldextract with network fallback disabled. Sources and SHA-256 hashes are in [data_sources.json](data_sources.json). URLs were processed as strings; no phishing destination was fetched or resolved.

The host-only pool has 19,772 eligible Tranco domains and 14 existing manual full URLs. Stable Tranco test buckets are excluded from this development run. After splitting, host-only training contains 45,929 phishing and 15,859 legitimate rows; validation contains 6,531 phishing and 2,016 legitimate rows across 6,131 registered domains. Cross-class domains are removed. The overlap audit is recorded in the JSON; train and validation groups are disjoint under the pinned PSL. Existing sealed rows were not evaluated or reassigned.

The full-URL experiment uses only the repository's 14 eligible manual full URLs: 11 training negatives and **3 validation negatives**. Tranco domains are not expanded into invented URL paths. Three negatives cannot measure a 1% or 0.1% operational false-positive rate. Its near-perfect point estimates below are therefore not deployment evidence.

| Variant / model | Recall ≤1% FPR | Recall ≤0.1% FPR | PR-AUC | ECE | CPU p50/p95 ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| host_only / char3_5_logistic | 76.11% | 18.59% | 0.9868 | 0.1676 | 1.18/1.72 |
| host_only / char3_5_calibrated_svm | 73.99% | 44.68% | 0.9883 | 0.0462 | 3.42/4.64 |
| host_only / lightgbm_hand_features | 84.11% | 79.88% | 0.9876 | 0.0999 | 1.61/2.09 |
| full_url / char3_5_logistic | 99.94% | 99.94% | 1.0000 | 0.0022 | 0.62/1.39 |
| full_url / char3_5_calibrated_svm | 99.66% | 99.66% | 1.0000 | 0.0003 | 1.91/3.17 |
| full_url / lightgbm_hand_features | 99.48% | 99.48% | 1.0000 | 0.0009 | 1.77/2.19 |

The selected host-only LightGBM has the highest validation recall at the 0.1% cap and sub-300 ms CPU p95. Its deployed threshold is **0.892338583898523**. This selection is validation-based, not a final-test result. The character and SVM alternatives remain packaged for comparison. Features include string length, digit/hyphen/dot counts, TLD, a label-count approximation of subdomain depth, IP/punycode flags, entropy and URL character indicators. For host-only inputs, path and query features are zero.

Selected-model 95% registered-domain bootstrap intervals (300 resamples): recall at ≤1% FPR 78.95%–88.75%; recall at ≤0.1% FPR 64.52%–85.03%. All models' intervals, PR-AUC, ECE and CPU timings are in [url_model_metrics.json](url_model_metrics.json). Thresholds are reselected within each bootstrap sample; these are intervals for validation-derived operating points, not an independent estimate of the fixed deployed threshold.

The timestamp experiment uses PhishTank max(submission, verification) timestamps and assigns whole registered domains by their latest timestamp. Cutoff: 2026-06-08T15:12:55+00:00; 25,312 phishing rows in earlier-domain training and 27,174 in later-domain validation. The character-logistic temporal stress test yields 22.81% recall at ≤1% FPR, 17.14% at ≤0.1%, PR-AUC 0.9788, ECE 0.5718. It is a domain-recency stress test: later-domain validation can include earlier messages from those domains, and the Tranco negatives are a current snapshot. It is not a reconstructed historical deployment or a temporal validation of the selected LightGBM model.

Tranco's popular domains differ substantially from phishing-feed hosts. Host-only features remove the obvious path-versus-no-path shortcut but do not remove source or popularity bias. Shared providers can host both benign and malicious content. The operational policy's suppression rules and its complete MEDIUM-or-HIGH recall require further independent validation. [The original 9,496-row comparison is explicitly marked NOT RUN](url_model_metrics.json).

## Task 3: frozen text comparison

All models use the train/validation row IDs, content hashes and near-duplicate groups in [text_split.json](text_split.json). The supplied rebuild is used without generating new synthetic training messages. Existing synthetic source groups, if present, are constrained to training by the repository rebuild. The validation cohort has 282 messages: 93 phishing and 189 legitimate. Language/source metrics use each model's overall validation-selected thresholds, so subgroup results do not retune thresholds on each subgroup.

The [selection rule](model_selection_rule.md) was written before computing this comparison: exclude CPU p95 >300 ms; retain models within 0.01 macro-F1 of the best remaining model; minimize legitimate FPR at ≥95% phishing recall; use the recorded simplicity order to break ties. ECE and model size are reported. Calibration is not an additional selection criterion. The old baseline's phishing-class F1 and the new macro-F1 at a different operating point are not directly comparable.

| char_tfidf_calibrated_svm | 0.9838 | 0.00% | 98.92% | 0.0610 | 5.27 | 1.39/2.59 |

**Provisional winner: character TF-IDF + logistic regression.** The original deployed text model was not replaced. Full per-language and per-source results, including the non-Egyptian templated Kaggle dialectal source, are in [text_local_metrics.json](text_local_metrics.json). The separately saved BiLSTM trainer report uses its original precision-oriented threshold; the table above consistently uses the v3 recall-oriented threshold. The SVM's internal calibration folds use training rows only and are not group-aware; the common main train/validation split remains group-disjoint.

The [transformer notebook](../../../notebooks/transformer_compare.ipynb) supports MARBERTv2 and CAMeLBERT-DA with three seeds each. No transformer fine-tuning was run here, as requested. It must use a **local Colab runtime**: hosted execution would send message data outside the machine and conflict with the hard privacy rule. The notebook checks row identities, hashes and split metadata, saves validation predictions and measures CPU batch-size-one latency including tokenization. It uses the first 256 tokenizer tokens, whereas local models use the repository's windowing, so context length is another comparison limitation. The merge CLI was run and refused the absent first prediction file; its successful merge path and duplicate rejection were tested with temporary local-model fixtures. No transformer performance is reported. See [transformer_status.json](transformer_status.json).

## Task 4: leave-one-source-out

Eligible sources have at least 100 phishing and 100 legitimate messages across train plus validation. For each run, the model trains on other sources' training rows; any group present in the excluded source is removed from training and threshold-selection validation. The threshold is selected on other-source validation. Evaluation uses **all development rows of the excluded source**, including its former training partition. No sealed test rows are included.

| Excluded source | Source development rows (phishing / legitimate) | Other-source validation macro-F1 | Excluded-source macro-F1 | Drop, percentage points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| kaggle_arabic_phishing_fully_dataset | 1025 (203 / 822) | 0.9702 | 0.8874 | 8.28 |
| kaggle_dialectal_arabic_sms | 289 (146 / 143) | 0.9875 | 0.3822 | 60.53 |
| salloum_gaber_bilingual_corpus | 261 (131 / 130) | 0.9848 | 0.8352 | 14.95 |

`kaggle_arabic_phishing_fully_dataset` is translated fraud/UCI-ham material. `salloum_gaber_bilingual_corpus` is English/Arabic parallel phishing-email material. These rows are translated/parallel sources, not authentic Arabic SMS. `kaggle_dialectal_arabic_sms` is non-Egyptian and templated, as identified in the project context. The drops are differences between other-source validation and excluded-source macro-F1; those sets have different class mixes, so this is a domain-shift diagnostic rather than a paired treatment effect. See [loso_text_metrics.json](loso_text_metrics.json).

## Limitations and preserved evidence

- Genuine Egyptian SMS test count is 0. Invented probes, translated emails and templated dialect material cannot establish Egyptian SMS performance. English remains unvalidated.
- Routine false alarms remain as MEDIUM. The decision rule sacrifices HIGH severity for many validation phishing messages.
- Popular Tranco hosts, source overlap removal, sparse manual full URLs and shared hosting limit URL generalization. Same-original-cohort integrated recall could not be established.
- Threshold tuning, model selection and confidence intervals all use exposed development validation. They do not constitute a sealed final test.
- Transformer training and final model selection are pending. No data were sent to hosted Colab or any analysis service.
- Local Windows checks do not establish the hosted GitHub CI result, Docker/PostgreSQL deployment or real-traffic accuracy.

[integrity.json](integrity.json) confirms the reviewed baseline files, original model artifacts/tokenizer/data and `evaluation/targets.json` are byte-identical to the supplied ZIP. Each new result JSON includes data/model hashes (null when no model applies) and library versions.
