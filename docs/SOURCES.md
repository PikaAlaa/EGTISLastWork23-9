## 1. Reviewer-Verified Facts (2026-11-26)

1. **Egypt Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020**
   - **Verified facts (2026-11-26):** Ratified **13 July 2020**, published **15 July 2020**, entered into force **17 October 2020** (source: Library of Congress *Global Legal Monitor*, published **2020-10-29**).
   - **ETIS usage:** Legal context for privacy-by-design minimization (hash-only scan/import indicators, no raw message/URL/payment-recipient persistence, and bounded retention). Hashing is documented as pseudonymization, not a claim of formal legal certification.
2. **Fawry Statement (26 November 2023) and Group-IB Case Study (Vendor Source)**
   - **Verified facts (2026-11-26):**
     - **Fawry statement of 26 November 2023 (`fawry.com` PDF):** Stated that the **production environment was not breached**; an **isolated testing environment** was breached, and it *"may have included the personal details of some customers"*.
     - **Group-IB case study (vendor source):** Reports the LockBit post on **8 November 2023**.
   - **ETIS usage:** Distinguishes primary company disclosure (`primary`) from third-party vendor reporting (`case study`) when referencing Egyptian fin-tech threat context in thesis background chapters.
3. **MITRE ATT&CK v20.0 Phishing Technique Distinction**
   - **Verified facts (2026-11-26, local hash-verified STIX 2.1 v20.0 catalogues):**
     - Enterprise `T1566.004` is **Spearphishing Voice** (voice calls, not SMS; never emitted for SMS).
     - **SMS phishing is Mobile `T1660`** (*Phishing*).
     - Enterprise `T1656` (*Impersonation*) is **revoked** in v20.0 (revoked by Enterprise `T1684.001` *Impersonation*). Enterprise `T1657` (*Financial Theft*) is never emitted by BEC payment rules v1 because a payment request is not evidence of completed financial theft.
